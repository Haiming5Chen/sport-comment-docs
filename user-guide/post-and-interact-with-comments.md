# Post and Interact with Comments

## Purpose

Posting comments allows you to participate in conversations about games and players.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure that:

- You are logged in
- You are on a player page

## Write a Comment

1. Click the input box at the bottom of the page labeled **Write a comment...**
2. Type your comment
3. Click **Submit**

## Reply to a Comment

1. Click the **conversation bubble** under the comment
2. Click the input box labeled **Write a reply...**
3. Type your reply
4. Click **Submit**

## Interact with a Comment

1. Click one of the arrows next to a comment to support or disagree with it

## Results

After posting or interacting with comments:

- Your comment appears in the thread
- Your reply appears under the selected comment
- Your interaction appears immediately
- New comments from other users may appear in real time or after refreshing the page
